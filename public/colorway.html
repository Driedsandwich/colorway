    Your results will appear here, each with a measured color match.

    Demo mode returns your uploaded photos unchanged, so scores describe the original photo and not a try-on.

    How to read this. The swatches compare your target color with the average color measured on the result. ΔE is the CIEDE2000 color difference; below about 2–3 it is hard to see by eye. The 0–100 match score is ColorWay’s own scale (100 at ΔE 0, falling in a straight line to 0 at ΔE 30), not an industry standard.
